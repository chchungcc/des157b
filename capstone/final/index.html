So let's review the facts.

Claim 1:

Immigrants are causing an increase in crime rates in the United States.

Multiple studies have found that there is no correlation between increased immigration and increased crime rates.


In fact, immigrants have never been more likely to be incarcerated than the US-born.

Claim 2:

Immigrants are taking jobs away from people born in the United States.

Immigrants create jobs by increasing consumer demand and starting businesses.


They fill labor shortages in manual-intensive industries that many US-born are often unwilling to take.

Claim 3:

Immigrants take advantage of government welfare support while not paying taxes.

Undocumented immigrants pay billions in taxes every year while being ineligible for government welfare due to their status.
