Capstone Proposal

Topic

The topic of my capstone project will be about immigration. In light of the recent socio-political atmosphere regarding immigration, I wanted to think about and address the future of immigration. Like many others, I come from a family of immigrants, and live in cities where immigrants make up a large portion of the population, businesses, community spaces, etc. It's no secret that America thrives upon the efforts of immigrants, and this is proven through history and society today. However, even with all of this history and successes surrounding immigrants, many people still have harmful misconceptions about them that recent leadership has been exaberating, further polarizing society and separating families. I personally cannot imagine a future in the US with no immigrants, and I cannot stand for families being separated by recent enforcement policies. In my project, I will be addressing common misconceptions about immigration and talking about how the future progresses with immigration.

Lenses

My project will be challenging the mental models that people have about immigration. I want to present these as questions and have the user answer them, similar to the ThanAverage project, and show the results from respondents and the actual numbers with data visualization. Furthermore, the main mental model I want to break is the idea of putting human worth based on their economic value, because this is often how American structures determines and defends groups. In my view, people have value by virtue of being human. They are full of endless possibilities that transcend capitalistic notions. I want to use this idea to remind people that immigrants are humans too, with lives, dreams, loves, of their own

Features

Since I have more of a questionaire style of narrative for my project, I am in between either having buttons that switch the screen after the question, or scrolling to change the screen. I thought it would be good to use the scroll on animate library if I were to choose the scrolling option. Additionally, since I imagine my narrative will have more text than images, I really want to try out different text animations through the GSAP library. I imagine this will make my project much more interactive and engaging, especially if it is more text-heavy. I also think that if I had the bandwidth for it, it would be cool to get anecdotes from immigrants to add onto my projects, and this would be present as little scraps of paper that users can drag and drop.

Concerns

My main concern is working with data from different respondents, as I am still not too familiar with storing and using data with JSON. I also need to do lots of research beforehand to ensure I am not perpetuating any harmful misconceptions of my own regarding immigration. As mentioned briefly in the previous section, I really want to add some personal notes or anecdotes from my community, but I need to figure out what to ask and how to go about getting this data. I want to make sure that I am not compromising anyone's privacy or safety as well.

Target Audience

My main target audience will be people who aren't used to being in communities of immigrants. These are people who might have met the occasional few immigrants, but are largely surrounded by homogenous native-born groups. It is these people who might fall more easily for propoganda against immigrants, so I really want this project to be a source of education for them.
